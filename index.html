I've spent over 20 years guiding documentation through every stage of a project's lifecycle — authoring, formatting, review coordination, revision control, and controlled distribution — working closely with subject matter experts across Engineering, Product, and Quality.

I've managed an array of technical documents, from API specifications to BI analytics documentation, translating technical, operational, engineering, and regulatory information into manuals, procedures, checklists, and training materials that are clear, accurate, and usable by the people who depend on them.

I bring in-depth experience with both Agile and Waterfall development lifecycles, structured authoring using DITA XML in XMetaL and easyDITA, and Component Content Management System (CCMS) tools — and a close understanding of how document control keeps teams working from the correct, approved, current version, especially when updates stem from engineering changes, regulatory requirements, or lessons learned.
